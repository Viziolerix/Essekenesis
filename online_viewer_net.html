Doctrine of Fusion

The Ordo System

An Ordo is a secondary school of Essekenesis applied as a modifier to a primary discipline. The result is not a combination — it is a third, entirely distinct art. Ten laws govern how this process functions, what it permits, and what it destroys.

Essence Vector Reference
Fusion Stability Classifications
Stable — compatible vectors, safe to attempt
Unstable — opposing vectors, risk of soul damage
Catastrophic — contradicting laws, severe environmental risk
Forbidden — dark Ordo on Light caster or vice versa (except Equasonae/Mortai)
Classification of Beings

Etherial Hierarchy

All beings capable of Essence manipulation exist within one of three Orders — Light, Dark, or Neutral. Each Order carries its own chain of classification, from the primordial lords of creation and destruction down to the unaligned mortals who carry only potential.
