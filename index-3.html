A Practitioner's Introduction

What is Essekenesis?

Essekenesis is the art of manipulating one's own Essence — the spiritual core of the soul itself. Rather than drawing power from external sources, practitioners shape, weaponize, and refine their internal Essence into specialized schools known as forms of Essekenesis, each governing different forces, concepts, or phenomena such as fire, gravity, memory, decay, or space.

The ability is typically ingrained at birth within Etherial beings and those connected to the Light Etherium, Dark Etherium, or Neutral classifications. Because every technique originates from the soul, overuse places immense strain on the user's Essence — risking corruption, instability, or complete spiritual collapse.

Advanced practitioners can fuse schools together through Ordo systems, creating hybrid disciplines with altered properties and exponentially greater destructive potential.

Doctrine of Fusion

The Ordo System

An Ordo is a secondary school of Essekenesis applied as a modifier to a primary discipline. The result is not a combination — it is a third, entirely distinct art. Ten laws govern how this process functions, what it permits, and what it destroys.

Classification of Beings

Etherial Hierarchy

All beings capable of Essence manipulation exist within one of three Orders — Light, Dark, or Neutral. Each Order carries its own chain of classification, from the primordial lords of creation and destruction down to the unaligned mortals who carry only potential.

THE LABYRINTH

Domain of Kaelthar · Essekenesis Miragae · The Labyrinth of Endless Lies

You are inside the Labyrinth of Endless Lies. Its passages do not stay where you left them. Its walls remember the shape of everyone who has ever doubted their own footsteps. Kaelthar built no true exit into this place, only the illusion of one, offered again and again, always one turn further than the last.

Recovered Accounts
The Cartographer
She entered with a compass, a lantern, and four hundred sheets of blank paper, certain that any maze could be mapped given enough patience. She filled every sheet. On the four hundred and first turn, the walls behind her had already rearranged themselves into the exact shape of the pages she had used. She understood, too late, that the Labyrinth had been mapping her instead.
The Twins
Two siblings entered holding hands, having agreed beforehand never to let go, no matter what the walls did. The corridor split so precisely down the center, so gently, that neither of them ever noticed the exact moment their hands had already come apart.
The Soldier
He swore he had counted every turn, and that he could retrace his steps exactly. He did, faithfully, for years, walking the same precise path back toward the entrance over and over. He never once considered that the entrance itself had long since become just another turn.
The Jade Dagger
Accounts describe a blade of deep jade said to carry an edge that does not distinguish between angelic and demonic Essence, a claim that, if true, would violate the immunity dividing Light and Dark Etherium entirely. No confirmed recovery of the dagger exists. Every account of its location places it deeper within the Labyrinth than the last account did, which some consider proof the weapon does not wish to be found, and others consider reason enough to doubt it exists at all. Kaelthar is not known to have forged it, nor to have hidden it. Only, perhaps, to allow the rumor of it to persist. A labyrinth is only as endless as the reasons people have to keep entering it.
